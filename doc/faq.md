## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Piano DSL?

Piano DSL is a domain-specific language for audio processing, GPU compute, and game engine development, backed by the HELLFORGE signing and security framework.

### What platforms are supported?

Windows, macOS, and Linux. GPU acceleration requires OpenGL 4.6+ or Vulkan 1.3+.

### How do I sign my plugins?

Generate a key with `piano sign --generate`, sign with `piano sign --plugin my.pkg`, and optionally submit for REGAS trust.

### What is the difference between TENTARI and REGAS?

TENTARI is locally trusted signing. REGAS adds server-side identity confirmation through the oshonet.in backend.

### Is HELLFORGE open source?

Yes. The Piano DSL compiler and all associated tools are open source under the Apache 2.0 license.
